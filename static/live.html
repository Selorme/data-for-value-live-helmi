Connecting

Send your Helmi on a trip

Every place on the trip has a bot that wants details before you arrive. You set rules for your Helmi. Then Helmi negotiates with each place in turn, and your phone buzzes when it needs you.

Everyone here is invented. No real personal data is used.

Set up Helmi

Which of your data may Helmi read?

Helmi never copies your data. It fetches a single line only when your rules allow. If you don’t connect a place, Helmi can’t fetch anything from it, and you sort those things out yourself on the trip.

What you wrote yourself (your needs) is always available to Helmi.

When Helmi isn’t sure

Sometimes Helmi can’t reach you in time: your phone is off, you’re asleep, you’re at a concert. If you don’t answer a question within 45 seconds, what should Helmi do?

Your rules for Helmi

What may Helmi share?

One rule per detail, for every place that asks. Ask me means your phone buzzes and you decide place by place.

The traveller and their data are made up. We keep an anonymous record of the choices you make here for our research: no names, nothing that identifies you.
